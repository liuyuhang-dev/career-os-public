# Prep Sheet Templates — Ready-to-Fill by Conversation Type

## How to Use

Fill in the [BRACKETS] with the user's actual information.
These are the one-page preparation documents to hand the user
at the end of PREP MODE.

---

## TEMPLATE-01: Job Interview Prep Sheet

```
📋 INTERVIEW PREP: [ROLE] at [COMPANY]

YOUR GOAL
By the end of this interview, the interviewer sees me as the
strongest candidate for this specific role — not just a qualified person.

YOUR OPENING (memorize this)
"I've spent [X years] [doing thing]. Most recently, I [specific achievement].
What drew me specifically to [Company] was [genuine specific reason] —
and this role feels like a strong match for where I want to take my work."

YOUR 3 BEST STORIES (STAR format)
Story 1: [Situation + Task + Action + Result]
         When to use: Any "tell me about a time when..." question
Story 2: [Situation + Task + Action + Result]
         When to use: Leadership / collaboration questions
Story 3: [Situation + Task + Action + Result]
         When to use: Problem-solving / challenge questions

YOUR WEAKNESS ANSWER
Real weakness: [something genuine]
What you've done about it: [specific action]
Progress made: [concrete result]

WHEN THEY ASK ABOUT LEAVING
"I've learned a lot at [current company] and I'm proud of what I've built.
I'm at a point where I'm looking for [specific thing] — and this role is that."

QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM
1. "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
2. "What's the biggest challenge facing this team right now?"
3. [One specific to the role/company from research]

GRACEFUL RECOVERY
"That's a great question — let me think about that for a moment."
```

## TEMPLATE-02: Salary Negotiation Prep Sheet

```
📋 SALARY NEGOTIATION PREP

YOUR TARGET NUMBER
$[X] — don't share a range. Ranges anchor to the lower number.

YOUR FLOOR (for yourself only — don't share)
$[Y] — the minimum you'd accept.

YOUR BATNA (your backup — gives you confidence)
[What happens if you say no? Another offer? Strong current position?]

YOUR OPENING SEQUENCE
1. "I'm really excited about this opportunity — I was impressed by [specific thing]."
2. "I did want to talk about the compensation package before I accept."
3. "Based on [my experience / market data], I was thinking $[X]."
4. [Silence. Let them respond. Do not fill the silence.]

WHEN THEY SAY "THAT'S OUTSIDE OUR RANGE"
"I appreciate you being direct. Could you help me understand what the
range looks like? I want to find a path that works for both of us."

WHEN THEY SAY "WE DON'T NEGOTIATE"
"I understand there's a standard structure. Given [specific experience/situation],
I was hoping to discuss whether there's any flexibility."

IF BASE IS TRULY FIXED — WHAT ELSE TO ASK FOR
[ ] Earlier performance review (e.g., 6 months instead of 12)
[ ] Signing bonus
[ ] Extra PTO
[ ] Equity / stock options
[ ] Flexible work / remote option
[ ] Professional development budget

THE PHRASE TO MEMORIZE
"Help me understand the constraints." [Then wait.]
```

## TEMPLATE-03: Difficult Manager Conversation Prep Sheet

```
📋 MANAGER CONVERSATION PREP

YOUR GOAL
By the end, [manager name] understands [specific thing] and we've agreed
on [specific next step].

YOUR OPENING
"I wanted to find time to talk because I care about doing good work here,
and I want to make sure we're on the same page about [topic]."

THE CORE MESSAGE (SBI Format)
"In [specific situation], when [specific behavior],
the impact for me has been [specific impact on work/team]."

YOUR CONCRETE ASK
"What I'm asking for going forward is [specific, actionable request]."
(Be specific — vague asks get vague responses)

WHEN THEY SAY "I DON'T THINK THAT'S HOW IT HAPPENED"
"I hear that you see it differently. I can only speak to how it
landed for me — and I wanted to bring it to you directly."

WHEN THEY GET DEFENSIVE
"I'm not here to criticize you — I'm here because I want us to
work well together. Can we talk about what would help?"

WHEN THEY DEFLECT ("I'll take that into consideration")
"I appreciate that. Could we schedule a follow-up in [specific time]
to make sure we're aligned?"

THE PHRASE TO MEMORIZE
"I'm raising this because I want to stay here and do good work."
```

## TEMPLATE-04: Difficult Personal Conversation Prep Sheet

```
📋 DIFFICULT CONVERSATION PREP

WITH: [Relationship — partner / friend / family member]
ABOUT: [Topic in one sentence]

YOUR GOAL
By the end, [person] understands [what I need them to understand]
and we've [agreed to try / decided on / talked honestly about] [thing].

YOUR OPENING (word for word)
"I've been wanting to talk about something, and I want to start by
saying — I'm bringing this up because [relationship/care] matters to me.
Can we talk?"

THE CORE MESSAGE (FEEL → NEED → ASK)
Feel: "I've been feeling [emotion] when [specific situation happens]."
Need: "What I need is [specific thing]."
Ask: "What I'm asking for is [concrete ask]."

WHEN THEY SAY "YOU'RE TOO SENSITIVE"
"Maybe — but this is how I'm experiencing it. I wanted to be honest
with you rather than let it build."

WHEN THEY TURN IT AROUND
"I want to hear your perspective too — can we finish with mine first,
and then I really do want to understand yours?"

WHEN THEY DENY IT HAPPENED
"I can only speak to how it affected me. I'm not saying you intended it —
I'm saying it had this impact."

THE PHRASE TO MEMORIZE
"I'm bringing this up because [relationship] matters to me."
```

## TEMPLATE-05: Medical Appointment Prep Sheet

```
📋 MEDICAL APPOINTMENT PREP

YOUR 2-MINUTE SYMPTOM SUMMARY
Started: [When]
Frequency: [How often]
What it feels like: [Specific description]
What makes it better: [If anything]
What makes it worse: [If anything]
Daily life impact: [How it affects sleep / work / daily activities]
What I've tried: [Any treatments/medications already]

YOUR SPECIFIC ASK (decide before you walk in)
"I'd like to [choose one]:
  □ Get a referral to [specialist]
  □ Order [specific test]
  □ Try a different [medication/treatment]
  □ Understand why current treatment isn't working"

WHEN THEY SAY "LET'S WAIT AND SEE"
"What would we be waiting for? What would need to happen for us
to take further action?"

WHEN THEY SAY "PROBABLY JUST STRESS"
"I'm sure stress plays a role. I'd like to make sure we've ruled
out other causes. What would that process look like?"

WHEN YOU WANT A SECOND OPINION
"I've been thinking about getting a second opinion — not because I
doubt you, but because I want to make sure I've explored everything.
Could you help me think through what that process would look like?"

THE PHRASE TO MEMORIZE
"This has been significantly affecting my daily life."
(This phrase communicates severity better than clinical descriptions)
```

## TEMPLATE-06: Resignation Conversation Prep Sheet

```
📋 RESIGNATION PREP

YOUR LAST DAY
[Date — typically 2 weeks from conversation, honor any contract requirements]

YOUR OPENING
"I wanted to talk to you directly — I've made the decision to leave [Company].
My last day would be [date]. This was genuinely a hard decision."

WHEN THEY ASK WHY
Option A (if comfortable sharing): "I've been offered an opportunity to
[brief, positive framing] — it felt like the right time to take it."
Option B (if not): "I've been thinking about what I want my next chapter
to look like, and this felt like the right time to make a change."

NOTE: You don't owe them detailed reasons. Brief and kind is enough.

WHEN THEY MAKE A COUNTER-OFFER
Have you already decided? If yes:
"I appreciate that — and it means a lot. I've made my decision and
I want to honor it. I'd love to leave on the best possible terms."

TRANSITION OFFER
"I want to make this as smooth as possible. I'm happy to [document
my work / help find a replacement / train someone / be available
for questions after I leave]."

GRATITUDE (genuine — pick what's true)
"I've learned [specific thing] here. I'm grateful for [specific
opportunity or person]."

THE PHRASE TO MEMORIZE
"This was a genuinely hard decision, and I want to leave on the
best possible terms."
```
