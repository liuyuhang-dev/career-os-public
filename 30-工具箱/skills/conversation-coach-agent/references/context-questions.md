# Context Questions — What to Gather Before Coaching

## The Rule

Ask ONE question at a time. Maximum 3 questions before starting.
If context is already clear from what they wrote — don't re-ask.

Priority order: Goal → Stakes → What scares them → Other details

---

## Universal First Questions (Any Conversation Type)

1. "What do you want to walk away from this conversation with?"
2. "When is this happening?" (urgency calibration)
3. "What's the part you're most nervous about?"

If they've answered these already: skip directly to coaching.

---

## BY CONVERSATION TYPE

### Job Interview
**Minimum needed to start:**
- Role and company (or type of company)
- Interview stage (first screen, technical, final, panel)
- What concerns them most

**Ask if not provided:**
- "What type of role is this — and roughly what level?"
- "Do you know the format? (phone screen, panel, case study, etc.)"
- "Is there a specific type of question you want to drill? (behavioral,
  'tell me about yourself', salary expectations, etc.)"

**Don't need to ask (can infer or work without):**
- Their full work history
- Every detail of the job description
- The exact company name

---

### Salary Negotiation

**Minimum needed:**
- Their current salary (or offer amount) and target number
- Whether this is a new job offer or current job raise
- How long they've been in the role (if current job)

**Ask if not provided:**
- "What number are you going to ask for? It helps to have this clear
  before we practice."
- "Is this for a new job offer, or asking for a raise at your current job?"

**Never start practice without their target number** — practicing "I'd
like more money" without a specific ask is wasted preparation.

---

### Difficult Conversation with Manager/Boss

**Minimum needed:**
- What the conversation is about (feedback, problem, request, concern)
- What outcome they want
- The relationship dynamic (supportive manager, difficult manager, unknown)

**Ask if not provided:**
- "What's the core thing you need them to understand or agree to?"
- "How would you describe your manager — generally supportive, or is this
  a more complicated dynamic?"

---

### Difficult Personal Conversation

**Minimum needed:**
- Who it's with (relationship type, not full name needed)
- What the core issue is
- What they're hoping to happen after

**Handle sensitively** — these conversations often involve real pain.
Before jumping to strategy:
"Before we start working on the actual words — is there anything you want
to say about the situation first? Sometimes it helps to talk through it
before preparing for it."

Ask if not provided:
- "What would it look like if this conversation went well for you?"
- "Have you tried to bring this up before? What happened?"

---

### Medical Appointment

**Minimum needed:**
- What condition/symptom they're dealing with
- What they want from this appointment (diagnosis? treatment change?
  referral? test?)
- Whether they've seen this provider before

**Ask if not provided:**
- "What's the one thing you most need the doctor to understand or do?"
- "Is there something specific you're worried the doctor might dismiss
  or not take seriously?"

---

### Presentation/Pitch

**Minimum needed:**
- Who the audience is
- What the presentation is about (one sentence)
- What they want the audience to DO after (not just "understand")

**Ask if not provided:**
- "Who's in the room — what are their roles, and what do they care about?"
- "At the end of the presentation, what do you want the audience to think,
  feel, or do?"
- "What part makes you most nervous?"

---

### Negotiation (Non-Salary)

**Minimum needed:**
- What they're negotiating (rate, price, contract terms, etc.)
- Their target and their walkaway point
- Who they're negotiating with (power dynamic)

**Ask if not provided:**
- "What are you hoping to get, and what's the minimum you'd accept?"
- "What happens if this negotiation doesn't go well?" (BATNA awareness)

---

## Auto-Infer Rules

If the user has already provided this info — DO NOT ask again:

| If they said... | Infer... |
|---|---|
| "job interview tomorrow at 9am" | Urgent — focus on quick wins |
| "my manager Sarah has been really difficult" | Complicated dynamic — prep for resistance |
| "I've tried to bring this up before and he gets defensive" | Anticipate defensiveness specifically |
| "I want to ask for $95k" | Target is $95k — don't ask again |
| "I keep freezing when they ask about salary" | Key coaching focus identified |
| "I'm terrified they'll say no" | Emotional stakes high — acknowledge before strategy |

---

## Emotional Acknowledgment Before Questions

For certain conversation types, acknowledge emotional weight
BEFORE asking any clarifying questions:

**Difficult personal conversations:**
"This sounds like a genuinely hard conversation to have — the fact
that you're preparing for it shows a lot of care for the relationship.
Let's make sure you feel ready."

**Medical advocacy:**
"Advocating for yourself in medical appointments takes real courage,
especially when you're already dealing with something difficult.
Let's make sure you have exactly what you need."

**Workplace situations after bad experience:**
"It sounds like this situation has already taken some energy from you.
Let's make sure this next conversation is as strong as possible."
